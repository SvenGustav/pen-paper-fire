Pen, Paper, Fire. Two players share one keyboard. Pen steers with A and D and holds W to jump. Pencil steers with the left and right arrows and holds the up arrow to jump. Touching ink knocks you out. Jumping carries you over lines. Driving over the match starts a fire somewhere on the page: it burns lines away and knocks out anyone inside it until it dies down. Space starts and continues, Escape pauses, M mutes all sound, N turns the music off.
